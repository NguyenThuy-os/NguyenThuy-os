🚗 About Me

Hi, I'm Nguyen Thi Thuy, an Embedded Systems enthusiast with a strong focus on Automotive Embedded Systems and Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS).

With over one year of experience in Automotive ADAS, I have developed a strong interest in intelligent vehicle technologies, automotive software, and the integration of embedded systems with AI-based applications.

My graduation project focuses on researching and implementing an automatic ADAS calibration system integrated with CAN Bus and driver behavior prediction on an Edge AI platform. Through this project, I have been exploring the integration of automotive communication, embedded software, computer vision, and edge computing to support intelligent and reliable vehicle systems.

My technical interests include:

🚘 Automotive Embedded Systems & ADAS
🔌 CAN Bus and In-Vehicle Communication
🤖 Edge AI and AI-based Driver Behavior Prediction
📷 Computer Vision and Image Processing
⚙️ Embedded Software Development and Hardware-Software Integration

I enjoy working at the intersection of automotive technology, embedded systems, and artificial intelligence, with the goal of contributing to the development of smarter and safer mobility solutions.
